CITEWEB (archive)
1998

> archive
CITEWEB was a free web host for French-language personal sites, run by volunteers from 1996 until it was sold in 1999. This archive keeps its 1998 redesign: the framed interface, the sign-up and hosting rules pages and the help pages (FTP, e-mail, IRC services...).

The original home page of this frame was not captured. The pages that needed the server (member area, forums, member directory, search, counters and forms) are not part of the archive.

> the 1997 site
The complete 1997 CITEWEB, with the sites it hosted, is at citeweb97.sauthier.family.
